Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

In short: Framatte processes your images on your own device and never uploads them. This website uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

1. Who we are

Framatte is provided by the independent developer behind Framatte. You can reach us at support@getframatte.com.

2. Images you open in Framatte

Both the free demo and the paid version render images locally in your browser. Your images are not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them.

3. Settings stored in your browser

Framatte saves your last used style (for example the chosen background and padding) in your browser's local storage so it is there next time. This stays on your device, contains no personal information, and can be removed by clearing your browser's site data.

4. Purchases

Payments are handled by Creem (creem.io), our reseller and Merchant of Record. Creem collects the information needed to process your order, such as your name, email address, billing country and payment details, under its own privacy policy. We never see or store your full card details.

Creem shares with us the order information we need to deliver the product and support you: your name, email address, country, the product bought, and the order date and amount. We use it only to deliver your download, answer support and refund requests, and keep the business records the law requires.

5. Emails you send us

If you email us, we keep the message and your email address for as long as needed to help you, and for up to two years afterwards for reference.

6. Website hosting

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and running. We do not use these logs to identify or profile visitors.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the providers named above (Creem for payments, and our hosting and email providers) and when the law requires it.

8. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing support@getframatte.com. We may need to keep some order records where the law requires it.

9. Children

Framatte is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
